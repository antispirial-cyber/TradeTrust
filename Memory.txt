TRADETRUST — MEMORY AND CONTEXT
================================

Read this first. It tells you why things are the way they are,
so you don't accidentally undo decisions that were made on purpose.


THE PROJECT IN PLAIN ENGLISH
-----------------------------
TradeTrust is a final year Mumbai University project (Sem 3, NEP 2020).
It covers two courses: Full Stack Java (FSJT) and Entrepreneurship Development.

The idea came from a real problem: bazaar traders in Mumbai extend informal
credit every day with no way to check if the other person is reliable.
CIBIL (India's credit bureau) only covers bank loans. The informal bazaar
world has nothing. TradeTrust is that something.

The tech stack is locked by the university syllabus. It cannot change
without written approval from the project coordinator. React + Spring Boot +
raw JDBC + MySQL + explicit Servlet classes. That is the full list.


WHY THINGS WERE DECIDED THIS WAY
----------------------------------
Score freeze during investigation, not score drop:
  A false report should not damage someone's public reputation. The platform
  must protect businesses from being falsely harmed. Score only changes after
  a human admin reviews and approves the complaint.

No public indicator during investigation:
  Same reason. If a pending complaint showed a warning on the public profile,
  it would be weaponizable. Anyone could damage a competitor by filing a
  fake complaint. Public sees nothing until admin decides.

Two round cap on counter-raises:
  Unlimited rounds invites abuse. Two rounds gives both sides a fair hearing
  and forces a resolution. After round 2, admin takes over.

Bidirectional reporting:
  Either party can report the other. A wholesaler can default too
  (failure to deliver, misrepresented goods). Fairness requires both directions.

Ledger is fully private, no sharing:
  Traders are protective of their financial data. Any hint of visibility
  kills adoption. The ledger is a personal tool only.

User-customizable accent color:
  Gives traders a sense of ownership over their profile. Small retention
  mechanic. Also makes the platform feel like a professional identity
  rather than a generic SaaS tool.

Past Records hidden behind score ring click:
  Reputation is led by the score, not the complaint list. The details
  are one step behind, which is how real-world reputation works.
  You see the summary first. You dig for the details if you want them.

Connect feature exists alongside complaints:
  A punishment-only registry has low adoption. People need a reason to
  register before they have a complaint to file. The directory and connect
  features make TradeTrust a professional network first.

No email or SMS notifications:
  JavaMail and SMS APIs are outside syllabus. In-app notifications are
  enough for academic evaluation and demonstrate the mechanism clearly.

Admin panel exists as a separate login:
  Human verification protects the platform from defamation liability.
  An automated approve system is a legal risk. The dev team is the admin in V1.


THINGS TO NEVER GET WRONG
--------------------------
MySQL port is 3306. Not 3036. The handwritten notes had a typo.
Always use: jdbc:mysql://localhost:3306/tradetrust_db

The score ring fill level is proportional to the score AND the color
changes with the score. Both change together. A 5/10 trader has a
half-filled ring in amber yellow. Not a full ring in yellow.

The glow on the score ring is not optional. It is the signature visual
of the platform. Box-shadow or drop-shadow using the ring color at 40%
opacity. If the ring has no glow, it looks like any other progress circle.

Logo is a PNG file with transparent background at /public/logo.png.
Reference it in code as /logo.png. Do not use text as the logo.


TERMINOLOGY
-----------
Trader         any registered user (Wholesaler or Retailer)
Cluster        a defined geographic market area (Zaveri Bazaar, Dadar Market)
Sector         type of goods (Fabrics, Ornaments, Electronics, Stationery)
Complaint      a filed report of a payment default or trade dispute
Counter-raise  a response filed by the reported party
Round          one cycle of complaint and counter (max 2 each side)
Escalation     what happens after round 2: goes to admin
Verdict        admin's final call: APPROVED or REJECTED
Past Records   the tab showing only APPROVED verified complaints
Score freeze   score held at last value while complaint is under investigation
Verified badge green badge for traders with clean record for 12+ months
Trust score    decimal 0.00 to 10.00 representing a trader's reputation
Connection     a manually accepted link between two traders

EXPANSION PLAN (documentation only, do not build)
--------------------------------------------------
V2: Multi-cluster support. Adjacent bazaar clusters in same city.
V3: Cross-cluster complaints. A defaulter in Cluster A visible in Cluster B.
V4: Sector-specific registries. Same engine, vertical filter views.
V5: Pan-Maharashtra informal trade network.

The V1 data model already supports V2 and V4 (cluster and sector columns
exist everywhere). V3 is one WHERE clause removal. The architecture scales
without rewriting anything.
