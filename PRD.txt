TRADETRUST — PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS
==================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
TradeTrust is a reputation and trust platform for bazaar traders in Mumbai.
Wholesalers and retailers deal in informal credit every day — goods handed over
with payment expected in 15 to 30 days. When someone defaults, the loss stays
with that one trader. Nobody else in the same market knows. TradeTrust fixes
that by giving traders a shared, searchable registry of who pays and who
doesn't — alongside a private ledger, a business directory, and a connect
network.

Think of it as LinkedIn crossed with a credit bureau, built specifically for
Mumbai's wholesale bazaar clusters.


THE PROBLEM IN ONE LINE
-----------------------
India's formal credit bureau (CIBIL) only tracks bank loans. Informal bazaar
trade credit — which runs into thousands of crores daily — is completely
untracked. Word of mouth only travels so far. TradeTrust makes it searchable.


WHO USES IT
-----------
WHOLESALER
  Extends credit to retailers. Highest need for risk checks before a deal.
  Can file complaints, receive complaints, connect with others, use ledger.

RETAILER
  Wants to build a portable reputation that travels with them beyond their
  local lane. Can do everything a Wholesaler can.

Note: Reporting goes both ways. A retailer can report a wholesaler too.
Either party can file against any other registered trader.

ADMIN
  That's us (the dev team). Separate login at /admin. Reviews complaint
  threads, approves or rejects them. Final say on all disputes.
  Not visible to traders at all.


PAGES
-----
1. LOGIN / REGISTER
   First page a visitor sees. Two tabs: Login and Register.
   Register collects: name, phone, business name, business description,
   role (Wholesaler or Retailer), cluster, sector, password.
   No OTP, no social login. Phone and password only.
   After login: go to Dashboard.

2. DASHBOARD
   Personal home after login. Three tabs:

   PROFILE tab
     Their shop photo, business name, description, trust score ring,
     verified badge if earned, list of connections, edit button.

   LEDGER tab
     Fully private. Table of credit entries: party name, amount, type
     (credit given or received), date (auto-fills today), description,
     status (pending / paid / overdue). Running balance shown at top.
     Add, edit, delete entries. Resets on refresh in V1.

   PAST RECORDS tab
     Only unlocks when user clicks the score ring.
     Shows all approved (verified) complaints involving this trader.
     Pending complaints never appear here.

3. BROWSE PAGE
   The directory. Search bar at top. Filters: Cluster, Sector, Role.
   Results are trader cards in a grid. Default: traders in same cluster
   sorted by trust score descending.
   Clicking a card goes to that trader's public profile.

4. PUBLIC PROFILE
   Another trader's page. Shows: shop photo, business name, description,
   score ring, verified badge, sector, cluster, connection count.
   Buttons: Connect, Report (flag icon).
   Past Records tab visible here showing only that trader's approved history.

5. NOTIFICATIONS
   Bell icon in navbar with unread count badge.
   Full page listing all notifications newest first.
   Types: complaint filed against you, counter-raise received, admin verdict,
   connection request received, connection request accepted, score updated,
   platform broadcast from admin.
   Mark all as read button.

6. SETTINGS
   Two sections only.
   Account Details: edit name, phone, business name, description,
   cluster, sector, photo, password.
   Appearance: pick an accent color (swatches + custom hex input).
   Color updates live across the whole UI. Saved per user.

7. ADMIN PANEL  (separate login at /admin)
   Queue of pending complaints. Click to expand full thread with all
   evidence. Two buttons: Approve / Reject. Confirmation before action.
   On approval: score updates, record added to Past Records, both
   parties notified. On rejection: score unfreezes at previous value,
   both parties notified.
   Admin can also broadcast a notification to all users.


TRUST SCORE
-----------
Every trader starts at 10.00. Score range is 0.00 to 10.00.

The score ring is a glowing SVG circle. Color follows a gradient:
  0.0  = deep red
  2.5  = orange
  5.0  = yellow
  7.5  = lime
  10.0 = vivid green
Color and ring fill level both change with the score. A 5/10 trader has
a half-filled yellow ring. The glow (drop shadow in same color) is the
signature visual of the platform.

When a complaint is filed: score FREEZES at its current value.
Public sees nothing different. No warning, no flag, no indicator.
Business image is protected until admin gives a verdict.

When admin approves: score drops based on disputed amount.
When admin rejects: score restores to pre-freeze value.

Verified badge: awarded when a trader has zero approved complaints
in the last 12 months and their account is older than 3 months.


COMPLAINT FLOW
--------------
Step 1: Reporter clicks Report on a public profile.
        Modal opens. Three steps: description + amount + date, then
        proof upload (JPG/PNG/PDF, max 5MB), then review and submit.
        Confirmation shown: "Complaint received. We will review it."
        Reported trader's score freezes.

Step 2: Reported trader gets notified. Sees the complaint on their
        dashboard with two options: Counter-Raise or Acknowledge.

Step 3: Counter-raise goes back to reporter. They can raise again.
        Maximum 2 rounds each side. After that, auto-escalates to admin.
        Both parties notified: "Escalated to admin for final review."

Step 4: Admin reviews full thread, approves or rejects.
        Both parties notified of verdict. Score updates accordingly.
        If approved: enters Past Records. If rejected: disappears.


CONNECT FLOW
------------
Click Connect on any public profile.
Receiver gets a notification. They accept or decline.
If accepted: both appear in each other's connections list.
Browse cards show mutual connection count if any.


WHAT IS NOT BUILT IN V1
------------------------
- Real-time anything (no WebSockets)
- Email or SMS notifications (in-app only)
- Payment processing
- OAuth or social login
- Auto-escalation timer (manual admin check instead)
- Cross-cluster search (V3 feature)
- Data export
